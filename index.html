Privacy Policy

Mi Grocery Lists · Effective 10 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mi Grocery Lists, provided by Veltiosi Kai Apps LLC (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you use the App.

In short

  • We don’t require an account, and we don’t ask for your name or email.
  • Your lists are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.
  • We don’t run our own servers that store your lists.
  • The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect certain device information.

1. Information we handle

Content you create. Grocery lists, item names, categories, and related settings. This stays on your device and in your personal iCloud account if iCloud is enabled. We do not have access to it.

iCloud sync. If you are signed in to iCloud, the App uses Apple’s CloudKit to sync your lists across your devices. This data is stored in your private iCloud database and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. We cannot view or retrieve it.

List sharing. If you share a list, the App uses CloudKit sharing. The people you invite can see and, depending on the permissions you set, edit that list. Your name or Apple Account identifier may be visible to them as provided by Apple’s sharing system. Only share lists with people you trust. You can stop sharing at any time from the share settings.

2. Advertising

The App displays ads using Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device type, operating system, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

  • If you have not granted tracking permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, ads are not personalized using your cross-app activity [and a non-personalized ad may be shown].
  • You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and you can limit ad personalization through Google’s ad settings.
  • Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, or payment information.

4. How we use information

We use the limited information described above only to provide list storage, syncing, and sharing, and to show ads. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your lists remain on your device and in your iCloud account until you delete them. Deleting the App removes local data. To remove iCloud data, delete the lists in the App or manage storage under iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage. Because we do not store your lists, we cannot delete them on your behalf. For ad-related data held by Google, use the controls linked in the Advertising section.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App’s ads or services, contact us and we will help address it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EEA, the UK, and California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information. Because we hold no personal data about you beyond what advertising partners process, most of these controls are in your device settings and the third-party links above. You can also contact us with any request.

8. Security

Your data is protected by Apple’s iOS and iCloud security. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted, and we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
